OpenArm v2 arm and pinch-gripper models

The RAI descriptions and mesh copies were taken from the OpenArm conversion in
vhartman/fixtureless-assembly, revision ddf6fff098f3fc32aecb4b445a72f8ae6158a40f.
The descriptions adapt joint/frame names for RAI, use primitive collision
geometry, and fix the pinch-gripper fingers open. They are kinematic planning
models, not calibrated hardware descriptions. Visual meshes retain their
relative paths; only files referenced by the left/right arm models are bundled.
